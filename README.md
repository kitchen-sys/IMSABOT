# John Hancock's Trading Empire

A historical web-based business simulation game set during the tumultuous years leading up to the American Revolution (1765-1775).

## Game Overview

Step into the shoes of John Hancock, one of the wealthiest merchants in colonial Boston. Navigate 10 historical events while managing your trading empire, making shrewd business decisions, and balancing profit with patriotism.

## Features

### Real-Time Economy System
- **Dynamic Pricing**: Commodity prices update every 3 seconds based on market conditions
- **5 Commodities**: Trade in Tea, Rum, Whale Oil, Textiles, and Furniture
- **Market Trends**: Watch prices rise and fall with visual indicators (▲▼)
- **Economic Reactions**: Historical events directly impact supply, demand, and pricing

### Historical Events (1765-1775)

1. **1765 - Stamp Act Crisis**: Colonial resistance begins
2. **1766 - Stamp Act Repealed**: Trade boom and market recovery
3. **1767 - Townshend Acts**: New taxes spark boycotts
4. **1768 - Liberty Incident**: Your ship gets seized by British customs!
5. **1770 - Boston Massacre**: Anti-British sentiment peaks
6. **1773 - Tea Act**: East India Company monopoly threatens merchants
7. **1773 - Boston Tea Party**: Tea dumped in harbor, scarcity follows
8. **1774 - Intolerable Acts**: Boston Port closes, economic stranglehold
9. **1774 - First Continental Congress**: Colonial unity forms
10. **1775 - Lexington and Concord**: Revolution begins!

### Gameplay Mechanics

#### Business Actions
- **Buy Goods**: Purchase commodities at current market prices
- **Sell Goods**: Liquidate inventory for profit
- **Smuggling**: High-risk, high-reward operations to avoid British taxes
  - 20-30% chance of getting caught
  - Successful smuggling increases reputation and profits
  - Getting caught results in fines and reputation loss
- **Fleet Expansion**: Buy ships to increase capacity and reputation

#### Game Stats
- **Treasury**: Your available capital
- **Reputation**: Standing among colonists (0-100%)
- **Fleet Size**: Number of ships you own
- **Inventory**: Goods stored in your warehouse

#### Winning Strategy
- Buy low, sell high as prices fluctuate
- Pay attention to historical events and their economic impacts
- Balance legal trade with profitable smuggling
- Build your fleet to increase reputation
- Maintain high reputation to unlock better opportunities

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. Read the introduction and click "Begin Your Empire"
3. Review the historical event for the current round
4. Monitor real-time price changes in the market ticker
5. Make business decisions:
   - Trade goods based on price trends
   - Consider smuggling for extra profit (if you dare!)
   - Invest in ships to grow your empire
6. Click "Advance to Next Event" when ready to progress
7. Complete all 10 rounds to see your final score

## Historical Accuracy

This game is based on real events and John Hancock's actual experiences:

- **John Hancock** (1737-1793) was indeed a wealthy Boston merchant who inherited his uncle's trading business
- The **Liberty Incident** (1768) really happened - British customs seized Hancock's sloop Liberty
- Hancock was a major figure in colonial resistance and helped fund the Revolution
- He became President of the Continental Congress and famously signed the Declaration of Independence with a large, bold signature
- The 10 events span the exact period from the Stamp Act to Lexington and Concord
