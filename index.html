Historical Vocabulary (1765-1775)

Learn the key terms from the Revolutionary Era

Patriot

A colonist who supported independence from Britain and opposed British taxation.

Loyalist (Tory)

A colonist who remained loyal to the British Crown during the Revolution.

Smuggling

Illegal importation of goods to avoid British taxes and trade restrictions.

Boycott

A coordinated refusal to buy British goods as protest against taxation.

Stamp Act (1765)

British law requiring colonists to pay tax on printed materials through special stamps.

Townshend Acts (1767)

Laws imposing duties on imported goods like tea, glass, and paper.

Sons of Liberty

Secret organization of patriots who organized resistance to British policies.

Customs

British officials who collected taxes on imported goods and enforced trade laws.

Duties

Taxes imposed on imported or exported goods.

East India Company

British trading company granted monopoly on tea sales to colonies in 1773.

Intolerable Acts (1774)

Punitive laws closing Boston Harbor and restricting colonial self-government.

Continental Congress

Assembly of delegates from colonies to coordinate resistance to Britain.

Minutemen

Colonial militia ready to fight at a minute's notice.

Merchant

A trader who buys and sells goods for profit, often owning ships and warehouses.

Embargo

Official ban on trade with a particular country or region.

Parliament

British legislative body that passed taxes and laws affecting the colonies.

Redcoats

Nickname for British soldiers due to their distinctive red uniforms.

Liberty

Freedom from oppressive rule; central ideal of the American Revolution.

Taxation Without Representation

Colonial complaint that they were taxed by Parliament without having representatives there.

Whaling

Important colonial industry hunting whales for oil used in lamps and manufacturing.

Molasses Act

Tax on molasses and sugar imported from non-British sources, widely evaded.

Committee of Correspondence

Network for sharing information about British actions between colonies.

Quartering

Requirement that colonists provide housing and supplies for British troops.

Proclamation Line of 1763

British restriction on colonial settlement west of Appalachian Mountains.
