📜 Historical Facts

The Real Paul Revere: Paul Revere (1735-1818) was a silversmith, engraver, and patriot who became famous for his midnight ride warning colonists of the approaching British forces.

Two if by sea: The famous "One if by land, two if by sea" signal lanterns were hung in the Old North Church steeple to warn Charlestown across the river.

Not alone: William Dawes and Samuel Prescott also rode that night. Prescott was the only one to reach Concord.

The famous phrase: Revere never actually shouted "The British are coming!" He said "The regulars are coming out!" since colonists still considered themselves British.

Captured: Paul Revere was actually captured by British patrol on his way to Concord, but his warnings had already spread throughout the countryside.
